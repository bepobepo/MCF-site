~ a community zine, in the making ~
CowZine!

A paper-and-ink hideout for the herd's thoughts, sketches, gossip, and grievances. We're putting together issue zero. We want your stuff.

~ what is this? ~

A zine by, for, and about the cows.

CowZine is a small printed-and-stapled (or beautifully photocopied) creature put together by the people who come to Mooncow Fest, and anyone else hanging around the herd. It's part-newspaper, part-art-mag, part-yearbook, part-classifieds. There's no theme, no party line, and no editorial committee — just a deadline and a pile of pages waiting to be filled.

~ open call ~

Pitch us anything.

We're especially looking for the things below — but if you've got a wilder idea, even better. Words, drawings, photos, scraps, half-finished thoughts. Everything's on the table.

no. 01
Personals

Missed connections, dating ads, romantic confessions, lonely-hearts mail.

no. 02
Letters to the editor

Rants, raves, gentle disagreements, fan mail, anonymous complaints.

no. 03
Opinion pieces

A hill you'd like to die on. Defend it, attack it, plant a flag.

no. 04
Reviews

A book, a film, a sandwich, a friend's haircut. Stars optional.

no. 05
Sketches & illustrations

Doodles, comics, portraits, marginalia. Black and white reproduces best.

no. 06
Travel articles

A place you went. A place you wish you'd gone. A walk around your block.

no. 07
News & investigative journalism

Tell us something true. Local, niche, or improbable. Bring receipts.

no. 08
Crosswords & puzzles

Cryptics, sudokus, mazes, riddles, wordsearches, dingbats. The weirder the better.

~ submit ~

Got an idea? Send it our way.

Pitch a rough thought, drop us a finished piece, or just say hi. We'll write back with deadlines, formats, and friendly encouragement.
